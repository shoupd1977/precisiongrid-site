Knock

Knock is a trick-taking card game in the family of Oh Hell and Spades. Up to six people play at one table, with computer opponents filling any open seats. Players bid on the tricks they expect to take, and the scoring rewards accurate bids. Ranked play tracks a skill rating across matches.
